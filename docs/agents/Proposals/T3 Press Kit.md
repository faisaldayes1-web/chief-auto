# Chief Auto: Dealership Simulator — press-kit text candidate

## One-line pitch

A comedy dealership simulator about finding the right car, fixing the wrong surprises and getting the deal across the desk.

## Overview

At OC Chief Auto, a promising auction listing is the start of the story. Buy cars, review their history, choose repairs and decide what belongs on the showroom floor. Buyers react to the vehicle's condition, and the sale continues through negotiation and paperwork rather than ending at an asking price.

Chief Auto: Dealership Simulator follows the dealership's core loop from auction PC to signed documents. Jeff can help on the showroom floor, while Marco offers dealership statistics, perks and market advice. Daily goals, rent, reputation and progression give each decision a place in the business.
