# Chief Auto: Dealership Simulator — store page candidate

## Short description

Buy cars, uncover their problems and work toward the sale. Run a comedy dealership through auctions, repairs, showroom negotiations and paperwork while building your reputation one deal at a time.

## About the game

The auction price looks good. The inspection report may tell a different story.

Chief Auto: Dealership Simulator puts you behind the desk at OC Chief Auto in the fictional Tewport Beach, Canioria. Find cars on the auction PC, weigh their condition against their asking price and decide which deal deserves a place on your lot.

Send cars to the garage and choose a mechanic for the work. Repairs affect the car your buyer actually receives, and hidden faults can change the negotiation. Set a showroom price, handle the buyer yourself or let Jeff try to close the deal.

Read the room during the sales conversation, watch the buyer's patience and get the paperwork right. Marco's advice, dealership statistics and changing market tips help you plan the next step while rent and daily goals keep the business moving.

- Auction buying with inspection and history information.
- Repairs and mechanic choices that affect a car's condition.
- Showroom pricing and buyer negotiations.
- Paperwork, reputation and dealership progression.
- A fictional setting with a comic dealership cast.
